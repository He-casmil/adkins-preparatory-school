Lower Primary

Warm, supportive classes where young learners build foundational literacy and numeracy, discover their interests, and develop confidence and love of learning.

Upper Primary

Rigorous, purposeful classes where learners deepen knowledge across subjects, develop independence, critical thinking, and responsibility for their learning.

Our Mission

To nurture every learner through warm relationships, purposeful teaching, and clear expectations that build confidence, character, knowledge, and responsibility in both Lower Primary and Upper Primary.

Our Vision

To be a school where every child is known and valued, challenged to do their best, and empowered to become confident, capable, respectful, and ready to contribute positively to their communities.

Why Families Choose Adkins

Parents want a school where children feel secure, are challenged to do their best, and learn to take responsibility. At Adkins, experienced teachers combine warmth with clear expectations, intentional teaching with joyful discovery, and individual care with high standards for all.

  • Warm, experienced teachers who know each child
  • Clear structures and purposeful routines
  • Gradual development of independence and responsibility
  • Regular communication with families

At Adkins, excellence is built through curiosity, discipline, and community. We are committed to every learner's progress.

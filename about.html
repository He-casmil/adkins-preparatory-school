Our story

A school built on care and character

Adkins combines nurturing relationships with disciplined learning so children feel secure, work hard, and grow into responsible young people.

The Adkins promise

We know that children thrive when adults are caring, consistent, and clear. Lower Primary and Upper Primary combine encouragement with high expectations and guidance that helps learners take responsibility.

Our Mission

To nurture confident, capable, responsible learners who love learning, contribute positively to their communities, and are prepared for secondary education and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a trusted school where every child is known, valued, and encouraged to pursue excellence; where teaching is purposeful and joyful; and where character and learning grow together.

Our Values

  • Respect and kindness toward ourselves and others
  • Integrity in all our actions
  • Responsibility for our learning and choices
  • Excellence through effort and perseverance

What Makes Us Different

Every child is known by their teachers, who provide personal attention, clear guidance, and encouragement. Our school culture emphasizes high expectations, positive relationships, meaningful learning, and cooperation.

Experienced Faculty

Experienced educators who know each child, model good character, and guide steady academic and personal progress.

Modern Facilities

Purposeful spaces that support focus, creativity, movement, and discovery.

Community Focus

We partner with families, keeping communication regular and celebrating shared successes in learning and growth.

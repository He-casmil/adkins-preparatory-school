Admission Process

Becoming a student at Adkins Preparatory School is simple and welcoming. We review each application thoughtfully and invite families to join our community.

1. Submit Inquiry

Complete the inquiry form to receive admission details and next steps.

2. Visit Campus

Schedule a tour or attend an open house to see our classrooms and meet our staff.

3. Share Records

Submit academic records, recommendations, and any standard application documents.

4. Confirm Enrollment

Receive your acceptance notification and complete enrollment paperwork.

Admission Eligibility

We welcome students who are ready to learn, grow, and contribute to a positive school community. Our admission team supports families through each step.

  • Pre-K through Grade 12 enrollment
  • Academic readiness review
  • Personal interview or parent meeting
  • Financial aid guidance available

More than academics

At Adkins, we believe a complete education includes sports, arts, clubs, community service, and friendships. Every child is encouraged to explore their interests, discover their talents, and become part of our vibrant school community.

Sports & Athletics

From team sports like football and netball to individual pursuits like athletics and swimming, our students develop fitness, teamwork, resilience, and healthy habits.

Arts & Music

Drama productions, music performances, visual art exhibitions, and creative projects showcase our learners' imagination and growing confidence in artistic expression.

Clubs & Activities

Debate, robotics, environmental club, science club, and more—our after-school programs let students pursue passions and develop new interests with friends.

Trips & Expeditions

Field trips, nature walks, educational visits, and outdoor adventures bring learning alive and create lasting memories while building confidence and independence.

Cultural Events

Celebrations, heritage days, intercultural sharing, and community events help us learn from and appreciate diverse cultures and traditions.

Community Service

Service projects and community involvement teach our learners to care for others and understand the importance of giving back and social responsibility.

What Makes Adkins Special

Our school is a place where every child belongs. Whether they're scoring goals on the sports field, performing on stage, working on a robotics project, or helping in the community, our students are encouraged to be themselves, take healthy risks, and celebrate their growth.

  • Inclusive opportunities for every learner
  • Experienced coaches and activity leaders
  • Well-equipped facilities for sports, arts, and activities
  • Strong emphasis on teamwork and character

At Adkins, we know that confident, resilient, well-rounded learners are built not just in the classroom, but through the experiences, friendships, and challenges they embrace beyond it.

Everyday School Life

Beyond organized activities, Adkins is a place where children thrive in daily interactions. Morning assemblies build community, lunch time is filled with laughter and play, and after-school moments create lasting friendships. Our school feels like a second home.

Classrooms as Communities

Each class is a tight-knit community where children look out for one another, celebrate successes together, and support each other through challenges.

Leadership Opportunities

Prefects, class representatives, and peer mentors develop leadership skills while helping to make Adkins a positive, respectful school environment.

Mentorship & Care

Teachers and older students mentor younger learners, creating a caring web of relationships that supports every child's growth and wellbeing.
